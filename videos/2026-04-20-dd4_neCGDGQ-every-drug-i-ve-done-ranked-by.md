## Summary

A first-person ranking of 22 psychoactive substances by perceived life damage moves from least to most harmful: psilocybin mushrooms, cannabis, salvia, spice, 2C-B, 25I, LSD, ketamine, MDMA/Molly, nitrous oxide/whippits, Adderall, cocaine/crack, alcohol, methamphetamine, Xanax/Klonopin, lean, Percocet/Dilaudid, heroin, fentanyl, with Suboxone as an honorable mention.
