## Summary

Russian officer Vladimir Valeryevich Dulyaninov, a commander in Russia’s 6th Guards Tank Regiment, alleges he was ordered to execute five soldiers who refused a Pokrovsk assault, then abandoned after being wounded during a supply-starved attack, crawling for six days on apples and tree bark before reaching Russia’s 24th Brigade, where his unit had reportedly listed him missing rather than evacuated him. The transcript ties his account to broader claims of Russian troop abandonment, starvation, extortion, and mistreatment in Pokrovsk and elsewhere, citing Focus Online, Kyiv Post, The Express, Ukraine’s HUR and SBU, The Independent, Meduza, UNN/UK intelligence, CEPA, Politico, The Guardian, Verstka, The Telegraph, and The Wall Street Journal, including Mikhail Simdyankin’s account of being ordered to charge a ruined factory. Key data and claims include Bakhmut losses of up to 30,000 by March 2023 and nearly 100,000 by June 2023, David Axe’s claim of 100,000 Russian casualties in Pokrovsk from January to October 2025, Zelenskyy’s October 2025 statement of 25,000 confirmed Russian deaths mostly in the Pokrovsk direction, 5,100 Russian deaths since January in the Dnipro delta, a reported killed-to-wounded ratio of 1:1.3, forced “company fund” payments of 150,000–200,000 rubles, alleged executions of refusing soldiers, and claims that wounded troops are often not evacuated or are returned to the front before healing.
