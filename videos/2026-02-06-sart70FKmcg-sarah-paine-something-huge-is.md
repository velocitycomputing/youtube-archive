## Summary

Sarah Paine argues that the post–Cold War “long peace” is ending because Russia and China, after periods of inward consolidation, are turning outward while the United States reacts to losing unipolar dominance; she names Putin, Xi Jinping, France/Europe, Zelensky/Ukraine, Iran, North Korea, Hitler, Japan/Pearl Harbor, Nord Stream 2, Greenland, ancient Athens, the Parthenon, Clausewitz, Sun Tzu, Michael Handel, and the Naval War College, and cites relative GDPs, China’s larger economic capacity, Africa’s double-digit growth during the long peace, and the risk that reckless leaders and rapid technological change make the moment resemble the 1930s.
