## Summary

The video claims Grok 4.6 launched yesterday and scored 61 on Artificial Analysis's intelligence index. That ties GPT 5.6 and sits one point behind Claude Fable 5. Grok 4.5 scored 56 a month earlier. The speaker says it costs about half as much as other frontier models, and about a tenth as much as Claude's top models for output. They also say it runs at roughly 100 tokens per second. They cite same-day blind tests where it tied Fable 5 overall and took first place on a cost-adjusted leaderboard, up from 14th. They add that it flagged fake problems in a trap test instead of inventing answers, and that Databricks gave it the top score on its office-document QA benchmark. The speaker credits agentic reinforcement learning for the gains and says there is a new "extra high" thinking level. They also point to live access to X as a unique advantage and say Elon Musk expects Grok 4.7 in a few weeks. They concede that Grok has no single polished app comparable to ChatGPT's or Claude's. Much of the video is a pitch for the presenter's paid "AI Profit Bot" agent-OS community, and the benchmark claims come from the presenter and Musk's own statements, not from anything verified in the video.

For you, Grok 4.6 is available through Grok Build, Cursor (half price until August 18), Grok Bot, and the API. If you already run an agent setup, the cheapest test is to swap it in as the model. Compare it against your current model on a few of your real tasks, such as document extraction, coding, or multi-step jobs. Start at a low thinking level and raise it only for hard problems. Check Artificial Analysis's published numbers yourself, and treat the "10% of the cost" and "number one" claims as unverified until you've run your own cost-per-task comparison. The X real-time access is worth trying if you do market or trend research. You can skip the paid AI Profit Bot, since the same swap-the-model approach works with any agent framework you already use. Also note the Cursor promo date, August 18, is already past as of today (October 9, 2026), so that discount likely no longer applies.
