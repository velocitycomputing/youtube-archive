## Summary

The speaker alleges that special prosecutor Lindsey Halligan had only days to review the case, lacked federal prosecutorial experience, acted under Donald Trump’s orders tied to a Truth Social post/email to Pam Bondi, and that the indictment was never actually presented to the grand jury that supposedly returned it.
