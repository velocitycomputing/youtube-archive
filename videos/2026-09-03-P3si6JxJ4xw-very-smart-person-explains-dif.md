## Summary

This is a comedic video where a "very smart person" and his sidekick, Honest Brandon, parody the stereotypes of seven genres: rock, metal, jazz, classical, neo soul, country, and pop. For each genre they cover the typical ensemble, song structure, and lyrical themes. They then list the supposed habits of its fan community and give joke "up-and-coming" artists, which are puns on real names (e.g. "Lead Zebra," "Iron Made in China," "Kilome Davis"). Brandon plays a short guitar demo for each. The video opens with self-promotion and ends with a pitch for Brandon's "52-week guitar player" program, which includes 56 modules with practice routines and two live Zoom streams per week. Little in the video is meant to be taken as advice, since it's satire. The only real call to action is the closing pitch for the 52-week guitar program, which is aimed at people who want a structured practice plan with routines, live feedback and accountability. If you play guitar, the demos are a quick way to hear the stylistic markers of each genre, such as rock solos, metal breakdowns, jazz comping and neo soul seventh and ninth chords.
