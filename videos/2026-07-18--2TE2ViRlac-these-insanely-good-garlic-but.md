## Summary

The video demonstrates how to make garlic butter steak bites using classical chef techniques. The chef uses bone-in rib steaks that are dry brined uncovered for 2-24 hours with salt, then cut into 1-inch cubes and seared in a smoking-hot cast iron pan with beef tallow for 90-120 seconds per side. The sauce combines a cognac or beef stock deglaze with shallots, minced garlic, cracked peppercorns, melted brown butter, lemon juice, and fresh parsley. Two sides are featured: mustardy potatoes (baby Yukons seared then finished with butter and whole grain mustard) and roasted broccoli florets (tossed with olive oil, salt, and pepper at 450°F for 15 minutes).

To replicate this at home, start by dry brining your steak choice (filet mignon, top sirloin, or New York strip work equally well) for 2-24 hours with 1.5-2 teaspoons of salt per steak. Cut into 1-inch cubes, sear in a screaming-hot cast iron skillet with high smoke-point oil for 90-120 seconds per side, then set aside. Deglaze the pan with cognac or beef stock, sauté shallots and garlic, add cracked peppercorns, melt in butter, finish with lemon and salt, then toss the beef back in with fresh parsley. Serve with the mustardy potatoes and roasted broccoli for a complete steakhouse-quality meal at home. The recipe is forgiving with ingredient amounts and timing.
