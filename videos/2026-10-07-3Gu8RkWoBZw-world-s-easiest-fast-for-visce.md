## Summary

The video outlines a 14-day progressive fasting schedule to reduce visceral fat (the inflammatory fat around organs). Days 1–3 use 14:10 intermittent fasting (14 hours fasting, 10-hour eating window), Days 4–6 shift to 16:8, Days 7–13 move to 18:6 (which triggers autophagy, a cellular repair process), and Day 14 culminates in a 24-hour water fast. During eating windows, keep total carbohydrates under 100 grams daily, eating only single-ingredient whole foods like eggs, chicken, beef, avocados, and olive oil. The fasting windows lower insulin, activate fat-burning, and during the 24-hour fast, produce a massive surge in human growth hormone (2,000% in men, 1,300% in women). The speaker also addresses questions about exercise, hunger management, artificial sweeteners, and electrolyte intake during fasts.

To execute this: follow the exact 14-day schedule, eating 2–3 meals daily within your eating window from high-protein, high-fat, single-ingredient foods (avoid processed foods). During fasts, drink water with sea salt or electrolytes, black coffee with butter/ghee, or green tea; no artificial sweeteners like aspartame or sucralose. When breaking your fast, eat protein and fat first, carbs last, to prevent blood sugar spikes. Start with walking only during the first few days; progress to burst or strength training as your body adapts. If hunger strikes, wait 10–15 minutes (hunger is a pulsatile hormone that passes), or consume a tablespoon of coconut or olive oil to stay satiated without breaking the fast.
