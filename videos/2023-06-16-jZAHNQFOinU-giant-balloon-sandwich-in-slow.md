## Summary

Gav and Dan of The Slow Mo Guys continue their annual "Giant Balloon June" tradition by sandwiching Dan between two giant water-filled balloons, a six-foot one underneath and a five-foot one on top. The plan was for Dan to stab the top balloon with a fork right after the bottom one burst, so both rips could be caught in a single slow-motion clip. The first attempt failed. The bottom balloon popped immediately but didn't fully release its water, and Dan ended up chasing a sagging, evading balloon. On the second attempt the top balloon was smaller and under more pressure, so it released everything at once. They saw something new in all their years of doing this: the rip travelled through the air bubble faster than it did through the water. The water then collapsed around Dan, leaving a clear shell. They found the balloon still intact and reusable, and they joked about next year's idea, which is Dan in a water balloon during a parachute jump, if someone has $18 million.
