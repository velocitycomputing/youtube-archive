## Summary

Paul, a US Army combat veteran, cites unconfirmed Euromaidan Press frontline reports that Russia’s three-week effort to complete pincers around Pokrovsk and Myrnohrad may be exhausting, with Russian control outside Rodynske and Novotoretske described as not decisive and contested areas around Myrnohrad, Nova Shakhtarske, and Mykolaiv difficult to map reliably. He argues that drone and artillery saturation has turned the fight into tiny infiltration groups—often three to four troops, sometimes a dozen or two dozen—making maps partly performative, while dense fog helped Russia for less than 10 days by degrading drone targeting. He contrasts Pokrovsk’s urban cover, which favored Russian infiltration, with Myrnohrad’s open fields, which expose assaults, and says Russia’s real prize would be an encirclement cutting Ukrainian forces between Krasny Lyman and Pokrovsk, because a closed trap could attrit troops through lack of water, food, and ammunition—citing roughly 72 hours without water and about a day of ammunition in heavy combat. He also cites ISW assessing that Russian ground assaults are struggling despite battlefield air interdiction by units such as Rubicon, and that Russia may ultimately take ground only through a costly attritional fight rather than a decisive encirclement.

For the user, the actionable move is to treat the “exhaustion” claim as provisional and verify it against ISW, Euromaidan Press, DeepState, geolocated footage, and official Ukrainian/Russian statements, especially because frontline maps and village control claims are highly contested. Track concrete indicators: weather/fog windows, drone availability, Russian assault sizes, whether Russia is shifting best units to logistics interdiction instead of ground assault, and whether Myrnohrad’s open terrain continues to prevent infiltration. Follow-up questions should ask which specific settlements are actually contested, whether Russia can close the Krasny Lyman–Pokrovsk trap or is only making incremental costly advances, whether Ukraine is withdrawing from indefensible terrain to preserve forces, and whether reported scorched-earth flattening is evidence of failure or a deliberate attrition strategy.
