## Summary

Piers Morgan interviews Noam Chomsky (then 94) in what appears to be mid-2023, covering existential risks and politics. Chomsky says the Doomsday Clock, now at 90 seconds to midnight, reflects two major threats: nuclear war and climate destruction. He argues the climate window is a couple of decades.
