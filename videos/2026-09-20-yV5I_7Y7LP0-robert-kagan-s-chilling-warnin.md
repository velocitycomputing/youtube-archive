## Summary

Robert Kagan and Bill Kristol argue that Trump’s second term is accelerating the collapse of the U.S.-led liberal world order, with the Iran war as a central accelerant: Kagan says the U.S. is in slow-motion retreat, Trump refused Saudi requests for help against Houthi attacks, Gulf states may be forced to accommodate Iran, and the conflict has damaged U.S. credibility and munitions capacity. He adds that Trump’s core movement is white supremacist, that European liberals are weak and misreading Trump, and that even a Democratic Congress may not stop a second-term president’s foreign policy.
