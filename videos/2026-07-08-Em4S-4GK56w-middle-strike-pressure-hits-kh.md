## Summary

Ukraine's Middle Strike campaign is severely disrupting Russian logistics in southwestern Kherson by destroying bridges over the North Crimea Canal and targeting the E105 and E97 highways, creating deadly chokepoints at Chaplinka and Colin Chakan where Russian military vehicles are repeatedly struck. Researcher Brendan Kelly details how Russian forces are forced into limited alternative routes while enduring 5,500–6,000 drone attacks weekly—escalated by Russia's recently deployed 50th Unmanned Systems Brigade—plus a new wave of Shahed and Gerbera strikes on civilian infrastructure. The result: 742 documented civilian deaths and 6,675 wounded since January 2024, with entire villages near the riverbank completely depopulated by the concentrated aerial campaign.
