## Summary

Brandon (a Canadian guitar teacher who runs the "52e guitar player" online program) lists 13 skills he considers the marks of an intermediate guitarist: muting with the fretting hand (essential in funk), palm muting, rakes (strumming across all strings but sounding only one, usually before a bend), and several bending techniques. For bending, he covers the Hendrick-style thumb-over-the-neck grip, bending with several fingers while moving the hand from the wrist rather than the finger, pre-bends (bend, pick, release) and unison/double-stop bends. The rest of the list is sliding, including sliding into a bend. It also covers hammer-ons and pull-offs (use fingertips, and flick the string on pull-offs), trills, two-finger tapping (with "Electric Sunrise" by Plini as a harder example), barre chords, and tremolo picking on a single string. The last items are intermediate fingerpicking and improvising over simple diatonic progressions like G–C–D–Em, even if you stay in one fretboard position. He closes by promoting his 52e guitar player waiting list and a $1 pack of 27 backing tracks, whose price he says will rise.

Use the list as a self-audit. Mark each of the 13 skills as solid or shaky, then spend practice time on the shaky ones. A few of his specific fixes are worth applying right away. For bends, switch to the thumb-over-neck grip, use multiple fingers to support the bending finger, and avoid bending under the strings or dragging adjacent strings on release. For rakes, make sure you can mute with your picking hand first, or the unplayed strings will ring. For hammer-ons and pull-offs, use fingertips instead of flat fingers and flick the string on pull-offs. He also says barre chords are the best grip-strength exercise, so practice an F barre chord and similar shapes. Treat the last 90 seconds as a sales pitch. The skill list is useful on its own, and you don't need the paid program to use it. The $1 backing-track bundle is optional and handy for practicing improvisation, though the "price going up soon" line is a standard marketing urgency tactic.
