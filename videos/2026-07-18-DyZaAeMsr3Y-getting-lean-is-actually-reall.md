## Summary

The video outlines a stripped-down system for achieving the "Fight Club" lean physique, rejecting industry complexity in favor of simplicity. The core is four levers: maintain a 300-500 calorie deficit eating whole foods, consume 0.8 grams of protein per pound of goal body weight (100-150g typically), train three days weekly focusing on compound movements (horizontal push/pull on Monday, squat/hip hinge on Wednesday, vertical pull/push on Friday) in the 6-10 rep range with progressive overload, and manage five S's (7-9 hours sleep, stress management, daily movement, adequate water intake, and sun exposure). Over a 12-week timeline, weeks 1-4 establish the foundation, weeks 5-8 accelerate results, and weeks 9-12 refine habits into a lasting identity.

To execute this: select three whole-food meals you enjoy and repeat them daily, dividing your calorie and protein targets equally across them. Start training immediately, picking compound movement variations that feel good in your body and progressively adding reps or weight week-to-week. Track progress through strength gains rather than scale weight (since you're building muscle while losing fat). Begin with whatever subset of the five S's is easiest—adding one each week—and commit to consistency over perfection for 12 weeks, at which point you should lose 10-20 pounds of fat if you stay disciplined.
