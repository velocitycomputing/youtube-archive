## Summary

Russia launched a massive overnight attack on Ukraine with 570 munitions—74 missiles and 496 drones—targeting Kyiv and other locations, killing 18+ people and wounding 90+. Ukraine's air defense intercepted 524 targets, but significantly more missiles penetrated than drones. Russia claims it hit defense manufacturing plants (including missile production facilities) and fuel infrastructure as retaliation for Ukrainian strikes on Russian oil refineries. The strikes appear motivated by domestic Russian messaging rather than genuine strategic advantage, as Russia has possessed this capability throughout the war. Unlike Ukraine, Russia rarely releases verification footage of successful strikes, fueling skepticism among Russian milbloggers about their effectiveness.

**Actionable:** Ukraine's President Zelenskyy has identified ballistic missile defense as the "absolute top priority," specifically requesting increased Patriot system production and joint licensing agreements with the US—worldwide demand for Patriots far exceeds supply. Russia's focus on gas stations is asymmetrical and less militarily effective than it appears; Ukraine has adapted by distributing fuel via mobile tankers rather than centralized depots near the front lines, whereas Russian oil refineries are fixed, high-value targets. Monitor whether the US increases Patriot licensing or production capacity, as this directly affects Ukraine's ability to defend against further large-scale missile barrages.
