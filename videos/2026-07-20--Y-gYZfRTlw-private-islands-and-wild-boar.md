## Summary

Jordan and Kaylee documented their van life journey in Costa Rica, including a tour to Tortuga Island in the Gulf of Nicoya where they encountered a wild boar named Tom that has lived on the island for over 20 years. They emphasized how Costa Rica offers pristine, untouched beaches and a chill vibe compared to typical tourist destinations. The couple also discussed practical van life challenges: their propane stove is running low, and getting a refill on the remote Nicoya Peninsula requires a 7-day wait to ship the tank to San Jose; additionally, they discovered their bus is too large for standard shipping containers and must use roll-on roll-off shipping, which is twice as expensive and poses security risks since the vehicle must remain unlocked during port storage.

For viewers considering van life or travel to Costa Rica: research propane availability and refill procedures before heading to remote areas—plan to exchange tanks rather than fill them, or arrange shipments well in advance. If relocating a vehicle internationally, get accurate dimensions early to budget for appropriate shipping methods and build security measures to protect valuables during transport. Worth-it experiences like Tortuga Island tours justify splurging on selective tourist activities, but focus on less-developed areas for better value and authentic experiences than typical tourist zones.
