## Summary

The documentary explores superintelligence as AI vastly outperforming humans across every domain, discussing the "alignment problem" of ensuring such systems serve human goals, and detailing the massive infrastructure required (data centers consuming 4%+ of US electricity). It covers job displacement—entry-level hiring down 19% for young workers—alongside genuine breakthroughs: AlphaFold enabling vaccine development, AI improving cancer detection by 12%, antibiotic discovery, and weather forecasting. The central concern: AI-generated personas can forge faces and voices reliably, enabling financial fraud and raising ethical questions about training on real performers without permission or compensation.

Verify any financial request or important communication by calling back through a known number or asking questions only the real person would know. Demand disclosure when encountering AI-generated content, and ensure creators whose work trained AI systems are compensated. In your career, prioritize skills that survive automation—judgment, taste, knowing which questions to ask—over routine tasks most vulnerable to replacement. While entry-level job displacement is real, so are genuine scientific and medical benefits; the actionable question is whether those gains are concentrated in few hands or broadly accessible.
