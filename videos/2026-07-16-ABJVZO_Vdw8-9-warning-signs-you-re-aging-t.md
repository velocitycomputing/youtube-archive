## Summary

The video identifies nine warning signs of accelerated aging after 60—persistent joint pain, difficulty rising from the floor, slowed walking speed, breathlessness during routine activities, poor single-leg balance, weakened grip strength, declining sleep quality, reduced resilience to illness, and unwanted body composition changes—and emphasizes that these are not inevitable but driven by weak muscles, deconditioning, and muscle loss that respond to proper training.

To reverse these signs, implement specific exercises: straight leg raises for joint pain, sit-to-stand exercises for floor mobility, heel raises for walking speed, step-ups for cardiovascular fitness, balance stance work for equilibrium, farmer's carries for grip strength, and the 3-2-1 method (three compound exercises, three sets each at 8/10 effort, twice weekly with weekly progression). Additionally, adopt sleep hygiene practices (no food 3 hours before bed, no fluids 2 hours before, no screens 1 hour before) and consistent strength training 2–3 times weekly to boost immunity and recovery.
