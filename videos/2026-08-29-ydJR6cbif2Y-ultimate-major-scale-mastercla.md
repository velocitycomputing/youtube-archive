## Summary

This masterclass covers seven progressive levels for mastering major scale improvisation on guitar. Levels 1–2 introduce the major scale as a movable shape and explain the seven modes (Ionian, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Aeolian, Locrian) as the same scale starting on different notes. Levels 3–6 present specific practice routines: playing all modes in all 12 keys, improvising over backing tracks, practicing modes in a fixed position across keys to test fretboard knowledge, and playing three notes per string patterns. The instructor emphasizes that modes are not theoretical abstractions but practical shapes for soloing anywhere on the fretboard, and that intermediate players struggle mainly from lack of repetition rather than lack of understanding.

To implement this, use the Random Scale Machine website to generate keys, set a metronome to a comfortable tempo (70–160 BPM for 16th notes), and practice each mode individually through all 12 keys while timing yourself with a stopwatch—tracking your times to measure improvement. Progress through one mode type daily (Dorian on day 1, Phrygian on day 2, etc.), then advance to playing all seven modes within each key. Once comfortable, isolate one position and play different modes staying in that fretboard location across all keys. Download the $1 backing track pack to practice improvising musically rather than just running scales. Commit to 14 days of daily practice and post your results in the comments for accountability.
