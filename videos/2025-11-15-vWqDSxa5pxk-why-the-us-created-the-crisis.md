## Summary

Host Ryan McBeth frames the U.S. Caribbean buildup as “Operation Southern Spear,” listing the USS Gerald R. Ford entering the Caribbean by November 10 with likely escorts USS Winston S. Churchill, USS Bainbridge, and USS Mahan, an air wing of 48 F/A-18 Super Hornets and six EA-18 Growlers, plus additional ships including USS Jason Dunham, USS Gravely, USS Stockdale, USS Lake Erie, USS Minneapolis-St. Paul, USS Iwo Jima, USS San Antonio, and USS Fort Lauderdale, with about 2,200 Marines. He claims U.S. strikes on Venezuela are imminent after the government shutdown ended but doubts a ground invasion, compares Venezuela’s military to pre-2003 Iraq, estimates 100,000–120,000 active troops and 220,000 paramilitaries, and notes Venezuelan assets: roughly 30 Su-30s, 18 F-16 Block 15s, two submarines, one frigate, and fast attack boats possibly carrying Iranian or Chinese anti-ship missiles with about 35 km range. He argues the official drug-interdiction rationale masks a broader U.S. effort to reassert hemispheric dominance against China and Russia, citing sanctions, the July 2024 Venezuelan election dispute where the opposition allegedly won 67%, Monroe Doctrine/Roosevelt Corollary history, Russian S-300 sales, and Chinese economic interests.

Actionable follow-ups: verify the deployment claims through U.S. Southern Command releases, Navy vessel trackers, and reputable defense reporting before treating “imminent strikes” as fact; research the July 2024 Venezuelan election records and opposition tally to assess the 67% claim; track China–Venezuela oil/loan ties and Russia–Venezuela arms/S-300 deployments; study Monroe Doctrine/Roosevelt Corollary and post-1990 U.S. intervention precedents to evaluate the geopolitical framing; consider escalation risks such as accidental clashes, aviation accidents from high operational tempo, and post-regime-change militia problems similar to Iraq. Useful questions: Is the buildup primarily deterrence or preparation for strikes? What legal/constitutional authority supports operations? What would be the end state for Venezuela’s 220,000 paramilitaries? How would China or Russia likely respond?
