## Summary

Bill Browder argued that Putin wants to win and cannot or will not stop the war, so US-Ukraine peace talks are “fantasy football” and Russia will reject Trump’s proposals, including a Donbas free economic zone, because without overpowering military consequences or credible security guarantees—unlike the failed 1994 Budapest memorandum—Ukraine remains exposed. He discussed Trump’s pressure on Zelensky to cede Donbas territory, share Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant revenue, hold elections or a referendum despite wartime constitutional limits, and accept a Kremlin-backed 28-point plan, while Germany, the UK and France prepared a 20-point counterproposal; he cited Trump’s 82% Ukrainians-want-peace claim, Zelensky’s conditional offer to hold elections within three months if voting is secured, US withdrawal of about 40% of military aid, a possible ammunition shortage by March or April, Russia’s ~1,000 daily casualties, over a million losses, 30 years of equipment lost, and Ukrainian drone strikes on a Lukoil Caspian platform (120,000 barrels/day, 20 wells suspended, 700 km away), four Moscow airports (disrupting ~200 flights), a shadow tanker off Africa, and an Odesa-detained cargo ship. He also referenced Trump’s Politico claim Russia has the upper hand, Wall Street Journal reporting on Trump-circle Russia business deals, US voting with Russia and North Korea at the UN, Mike Pompeo’s and Henry Bolton’s warnings about appeasement, and Belgium/Euroclear legal disputes over roughly €140 billion in frozen Russian assets, which he predicted the EU would seize on Thursday.
