## Summary

Olivia Rodrigo performed four songs from her album "guts" at NPR's Tiny Desk Concert, discussing her creative process for each track. "Love is embarrassing" came from a single night of self-reflection, "vampire" underwent two months of meticulous production refinement with her producer (particularly on BPM and chord voicings), "lacy" originated from a USC poetry assignment where she experimented by writing full lyrics before adding melody, and "making the bed" explores accountability and the consequences of poor relationship choices—a central theme of the album.

Listen to the concert and "guts" album to hear the polished final versions and understand her songwriting progression. If you're interested in songwriting, adopt her approach of starting with pure lyrical or poetic writing and layering melody afterward (rather than composing simultaneously), and invest in detailed production refinement rather than settling for quick drafts—her two-month polish on "vampire" demonstrates how meticulous attention to small details elevates a song.
