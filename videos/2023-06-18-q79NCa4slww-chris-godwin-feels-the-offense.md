## Summary

Chris Godwin, speaking at Buccaneers minicamp after the first day, said there is a lot of work and growth ahead, which he called normal for June. The team is focused on playing physical, efficient, complementary football, with no fixed run-or-pass identity. He said he is confident in the team's ability to take downfield shots if a game calls for it, and he likes the running game. He described the roster as far younger than in the past and expects it to be one of the league's youngest. He called that energizing, though mistakes will come. He is healthier than last year, so he can work on his game instead of only rehabbing his leg. He wants to improve his route efficiency (the tops of routes and releases) and his run after the catch, which fell off last year. He said the new offense means learning new terminology, but he thinks the coaches are making it digestible and not harping on mistakes. The quarterback's launch point is different, and he expects more scramble plays and more creativity. He praised Mike (Evans), who is entering his 10th season, as the ultimate pro and one of the league's most underrated players. He credited Tristan (Wirfs) with stepping up as a vocal leader and said both quarterbacks are leading the huddle well. He singled out improvement from Zion and the addition of Ryan Neal on defense. He described the offensive coordinator as energetic and a student of the game.
